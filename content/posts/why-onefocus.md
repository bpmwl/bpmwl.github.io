AI priorities change too quickly for a static profile.

OneFocus is a place to keep the models, tools, and ideas that matter **right now** — with enough context to remember why they mattered.

## What belongs here

- Models I actively use
- Tools I am testing
- Short notes from ongoing work
- Longer posts when an idea needs room

Everything is stored in GitHub, so the site stays portable and the history stays visible.
